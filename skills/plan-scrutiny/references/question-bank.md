# Question bank

Pick four to six per message. Ask in the user's vocabulary. Skip anything the conversation already answers. The compressed set (three per block) is marked with an asterisk.

## Framing (first message)

- Who will review this, and what have they sent back before?
- What is already decided and not up for discussion?
- What format do they expect (one page, a deck, a flow, a document)?
- What is the deadline, and what is the one decision you need from them?
- What happened the last time a plan like this was reviewed?

## For every step in a flow

- *Who does this step, by name, and who is the backup?
- *What comes in, from where, and who provides it?
- *What happens when this step fails, stalls or the input does not arrive?
- What comes out, and who receives it?
- Is this done today? If yes, how, and what is its limit?
- Is anything here automatic? If yes, what stops it from doing the wrong thing?
- How long does it wait before the next person touches it?
- Say this step in one sentence a new hire would understand.

## For every decision point or branch

- What are the branches, and does each one lead to a step?
- Who decides which branch, and from what information?
- What is the unhappy branch, and what happens on it?
- What happens when the answer is "neither" or "not yet"?

## For every role or person

- What do they own, in one line?
- What do they do in this flow, step by step?
- Who backs them up, and has the backup agreed?
- What happens in the first 48 hours if they leave?
- Who else touches this that is not on the page yet (client side, finance, legal, ops)?

## For every tool or system

- What is its real name, and who administers it?
- What does it do today, and what is its limit?
- What access does it need, who grants it, and what if it is refused?
- What does the plan do by hand if the tool is down for a week?
- What is the cost, and who approved it?

## For every rule or automation

- When exactly does it fire?
- What does it do, and who is told?
- Who owns the rule, and who changes it?
- Can it send anything outside the company or change a record? If yes, who approves before it does?

## For every recommendation

- What is the logic, in two or three sentences, that someone could apply to a case not on your list?
- What did you reject, and what was the best argument for it?
- What evidence or number would change your mind?
- Who agreed to this, and where is that written?

## For the plan as a whole

- Tell me the story of how this fails six months from now.
- What is measured today, and what will be measured in 30 days?
- When is this reviewed, by whom, and what input does the review read?
- Where does feedback go, and who turns it into a change?
- Which sections share a variable (a mode, a role, a region), and does every section treat it the same way?
- What would you cut if the reviewers said it was too complicated?

## Vague-answer follow-ups

Use when the answer is "the team will handle it", "we'll figure it out", "standard process":
- Who on the team, by name?
- By when, and what tells them it is their turn?
- What do they need in hand to do it?
- What happens if they do not?
- Has this been done before here? What happened?

## "You decide" follow-ups

Never decide silently. Offer:
- Option A: ... (trade-off in one line)
- Option B: ... (trade-off in one line)
- Option C, if there is one.
Then: "Which one, and in one sentence, why?" Record the answer as the user's reasoning.
